Prologue

A meme coin,
born to become a key.

HYPERION was minted on Robinhood Chain through Pons — a fixed supply of one billion, fully on-chain, owned by no one but its holders. Named after the Titan of light, it began as an experiment in creation. It is now being forged into a utility: the access credential of a personal MCP tool suite.

The Roadmap

Three phases.
From token to key.

Phase I — Founding Snapshot

Holder addresses are recorded on-chain. Early holders are whitelisted as founding members of the tool suite.

Phase II — Token-Gated Access

MCP endpoints read the caller's wallet. Holding HYPERION is what opens the doors — the coin becomes the key.

Phase III — Pay-with-Token

Usage quotas settle in HYPERION. The credential becomes the native payment rail of the tools it unlocks.
