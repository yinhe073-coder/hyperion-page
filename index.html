What is HYPERION?

HYPERION is a community meme coin launched on Robinhood Chain via Pons. It began as an experiment in token creation — and it is being developed into something more: a utility credential.

  • Issued on Robinhood Chain through the Pons launch platform
  • Fixed supply, fully on-chain, no pre-minted team unlock
  • Named after the Titan of light — the token that lights the way into tooling

The MCP Credential Roadmap (Planned)

The founder is building a personal suite of MCP (Model Context Protocol) tools. HYPERION is planned to become the access credential for that suite:

Phase 1 — Airdrop Snapshot

Holder addresses are snapshotted on-chain. Early holders are whitelisted as founding members.

Phase 2 — Token-Gated Access

MCP endpoints read your wallet: holding HYPERION grants usage tiers of the tool suite.

Phase 3 — Pay-with-Token

Usage quotas are settled in HYPERION — the coin becomes the native payment rail of the tools.

All utility plans are forward-looking development goals. They are not promised returns, and no roadmap item creates an obligation.

Holding Tiers

  • Bronze — any holder: public tool endpoints, free tier
  • Silver — top holders: priority queue + higher daily quota
  • Gold — founding snapshot: lifetime founder access, feature voting
